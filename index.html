A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before we
hit play.

What's the difference between Enhanced and Signature?

Enhanced focuses on pacing, branded captions, B-roll, animated text, and sound design. Signature adds more involved custom motion, compositing, and visual sequences. We'll agree a reference and complexity level before production.

How quickly will I receive my edits?

A standard individual reel typically takes two business days once I have the complete brief and assets. Monthly packages follow an agreed batch schedule. Feedback timing, revision requests, and complex motion can affect the final delivery date.

What do I need to provide?

Your footage, brand assets, reference examples, and the message you want to communicate. For scripting packages, provide the topic and key points. Idea research and filming aren't included.

How do revisions work?

Every reel includes three rounds of consolidated feedback within the original brief. A new concept, new footage, or a change in scope may need a separate quote.

Can you work under my agency's brand?

Yes. White-label delivery is available across all packages. I follow your brand guidelines and organize final exports for you to share with your clients.

Can we start with a single project?

Absolutely. Send me the brief, footage length, and a reference for the editing style. I'll put together a quote for the scope before we start.
