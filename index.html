A few questions

What's the difference between Enhanced and Signature?

Enhanced covers captions, B-roll, animated text, and sound. Signature includes more involved motion graphics and compositing. We agree the style and scope before starting.

How quickly will I receive my edits?

A standard individual reel typically takes two business days once I have the complete brief and assets. Monthly packages follow an agreed batch schedule. Feedback timing, revision requests, and complex motion can affect the final delivery date.

What do I need to provide?

Footage, brand assets, and a style reference. For scripting, send your topic and key points. Research and filming are not included.

How do revisions work?

Three rounds per reel, within the agreed brief. New concepts or replacement footage may need a separate quote.

Can you work under my agency's brand?

Yes, across all packages. I follow your guidelines and deliver exports for you to share with clients.

Can we start with a single project?

Yes. Send a brief and style reference for a one-off quote.
